### Related to `Different Ceramic Structures.pdf` file:

- NaCl [Rock Salt Structure]
    - Lattice Parameter:  
    a = √2 (r<sub>c</sub> + r<sub>a</sub>)
    - n<sub>A</sub> (1/8 * 8) + (6 * 1/2) = 4  
      n<sub>C</sub> (12 * 1/4) + (1) = 4

- ZnS [Zinc Blende Structure]
    - Lattice Parameter:  
    a = <sup>4</sup>&frasl;<sub>√3</sub> (r<sub>c</sub> + r<sub>a</sub>)

- CaF<sub>2</sub> [Flourite Structure]
    - Lattice Parameter:  
    a = <sup>4</sup>&frasl;<sub>√3</sub> (r<sub>c</sub> + r<sub>a</sub>)

> [!NOTE]
> ZnS and CaF₂ look different chemically, but geometrically they are both FCC frameworks stabilized by tetrahedral site filling, leading to similar space-filling efficiency and lattice scaling.
